# Battery Management System (BMS)

For a 2S2P battery pack, a BMS is essential to keep the pack safe, stable, and reliable.

## Why use a BMS?

- Protects cells from overcharge, overdischarge, overcurrent, and short-circuit events.
- Balances cell groups to improve pack lifetime and maintain consistent performance.
- Monitors voltage and current so the system can operate within safe limits.

In short: a BMS reduces risk, increases battery life, and improves overall system reliability.
